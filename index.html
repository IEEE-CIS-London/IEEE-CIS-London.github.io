    About the chapter

    A local home for the IEEE CIS community

    The IEEE Computational Intelligence Society is the IEEE's professional home for the theory, design and application of biologically and linguistically motivated computational paradigms. Our chapter brings that community to London, Ontario and the surrounding region as part of the IEEE London Section.

    Chapter activities are open to IEEE members and to anyone interested in the field — students, academics and industry practitioners alike.

    • Talks & Distinguished Lectures

      Seminars by local researchers and visiting speakers, including the IEEE CIS Distinguished Lecturer Program.

    • Workshops & tutorials

      Hands-on sessions on methods and tools, from foundations to current research practice.

    • Students & early careers

      Networking, mentoring and opportunities to present work for students and young professionals.

    Research areas

    The fields we cover

    The three core technical areas of IEEE CIS, plus the emerging topics around them.

    • NN

      Neural Networks

      Deep and representation learning, learning theory, neuromorphic and spiking models, and their applications.

      IEEE TNNLS ↗︎
    • FS

      Fuzzy Systems

      Fuzzy logic and inference, uncertainty modelling, and interpretable, human-centric reasoning.

      IEEE TFS ↗︎
    • EC

      Evolutionary Computation

      Evolutionary algorithms, swarm intelligence, and multi-objective and black-box optimization.

      IEEE TEVC ↗︎
    • AI

      Emerging AI Topics

      Trustworthy and explainable AI, hybrid and agentic systems, and computational intelligence in practice.

      IEEE TAI ↗︎
